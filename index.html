About

Short version

I finished my Experimental Sciences diploma with a 20.0/20.0 average and immediately went looking for the part of science that lets you build things. That turned out to be systems programming. I now spend most of my time in a Linux terminal writing Python and learning Rust, because I want to understand machines at the level where the abstractions stop hiding things.

My goal is specific: a BSc in Computer Science at a German technical university — TUM, RWTH Aachen or KIT. I chose Germany deliberately, for the depth of its engineering tradition and the fact that its universities expect you to actually understand the mathematics. I’ve been learning German for that reason and currently sit at B2.

Outside of that I train calisthenics and play competitive volleyball. Both taught me the same thing programming did: you get good at something by doing the boring repetitions long after the novelty wears off.
