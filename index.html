Problem
Diagnosing a network meant running four utilities and correlating their output by eye.
Approach
A single Python command-line tool that runs host discovery, port scanning and latency probes concurrently on asyncio, then prints one report. Standard library only.

Open questions

Not built. Deliberately.

These are the problems the degree is for. Listing them as products would be a lie; listing them as questions is the honest version and, for an academic reader, the more interesting one.

Palm-contact biosignals

Concept

How much is genuinely recoverable from photoplethysmography at a single hand contact before the noise floor wins? That is a signal-processing question, and I do not yet have the mathematics for it.

Alignment at small scale

Concept

Needleman–Wunsch is quadratic and every practical tool cheats around it. I want to implement the exact algorithm first, badly and slowly, before I am allowed to use the fast one.

Moython

In progress
